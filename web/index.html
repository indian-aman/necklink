03 · CERVICAL CONNECTIVE & VENTRAL NERVE CORD

3,600 neck axons distilled into 10 descending channels & 4 wire bytes.

Every insect reflex crosses a physical bottleneck: the cervical neck. NeckLink CNS compiles 11 brain populations onto 10 core descending channels (DNp01 giant fiber, MDN reverse, DNa02 contralateral turn) driving a 6×6 premotor inhibition matrix in the Ventral Nerve Cord.

With zero hardcoded phase table, the real VNC matrix spontaneously locks 6 limbs into an alternating tripod gait (order 0.855 vs 0.411 across 100 shuffles, p = 0.0099), serialized across boards as a 4-byte CRC-4 cervical frame or loss-healed over NLTP v1.6.0.

Audit methodology, Honest Nulls & where classical algorithms win

All 6 experiments reside in benchmarks/v3/ with frozen raw BANC v888 subgraphs (banc_v888_raw_subgraphs.pkl.gz) and exact two-sided permutation p = (r + 1)/(K + 1) at K = 100. Where topology matters: Central Complex homing (100% vs 4.7%), ring stability (12.84° vs 62.82°), emergent VNC tripod gait (0.855 vs 0.411), and closed-loop USV hazard survival (66.7% 30-seed / 58.3% vs 4.6% shuffle gate). Where it does not: Mushroom Body Kenyon claws match random degree-matched projections (0.666 vs 0.672, consistent with Dasgupta et al., 2017), and v2_analytic or classical χ² EKF / One-Class SVM achieve higher engineering accuracy on clean sensors.
